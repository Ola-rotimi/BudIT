BudT Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

In short
  • BudT has no accounts and doesn’t collect your personal or financial data.
  • Everything you enter stays in a database on your device.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • BudT sends two kinds of request over the internet, and neither includes anything you’ve entered: official exchange rates, and BudT Pro purchase checks (handled by RevenueCat).

Who we are

BudT is a personal budgeting app for iOS and Android, developed by Olarotimi Adamson (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

BudT stores what you enter only on your device. This includes:

  • transactions
  • categories
  • budgets
  • savings goals
  • bills
  • accounts and wallets, transfers and recurring entries
  • debts and savings groups (ajo, esusu, chama and similar)
  • your settings, such as country, currency and theme

We don’t operate servers that receive this data, and we can’t see, access or recover it.

How your data is protected

  • Encryption on your device. In versions installed from the App Store or Google Play, BudT’s database is encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). The encryption key is generated on your device and kept in its secure keystore (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • App lock. You can require a PIN, and optionally Face ID or a fingerprint, to open BudT. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash in the secure keystore, never in plain text. Face ID and fingerprint checks are carried out by your phone’s operating system; BudT never receives your biometric data.
  • Privacy screen and hidden amounts. You can hide BudT’s contents in the recent-apps view and block screenshots, and mask balances on screen.

The tax, pension, loan and savings calculators run entirely on your device. Nothing you type into them is saved or sent anywhere.

Information we collect

We don’t collect your personal or financial data. BudT doesn’t include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it doesn’t ask for an account, email address or phone number. The only data handled off your device relates to BudT Pro purchases, described below.

BudT Pro purchases

BudT Pro is sold through the App Store and Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment, and we never see your card or payment details.

To check whether you have Pro, BudT uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier created for your installation, not your name or email
  • your purchase and subscription history for BudT
  • basic device information, such as platform, app version, country/store region and IP address

This information is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and give us anonymous sales totals. It isn’t used for advertising or tracking, and it’s never combined with what you’ve entered in BudT.

Statement import, reminders and summaries

  • Statement import. When you import a bank or mobile-money statement, BudT reads the file you choose on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere, and BudT deletes its temporary copy after reading it.
  • Reminders. Bill and daily reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. They don’t go through any server. While the app lock or hidden amounts is on, reminders leave out amounts so they don’t show on your lock screen.
  • Annual summary. The PDF summary is created on your device and only goes where you choose to share it.

Exchange rates

The currency converter and the “≈ in your currency” figures use official central bank exchange rates. To get them, BudT requests the rates from Frankfurter, an open-source service that republishes rates from central banks.

A request contains only:

  • the currency codes involved, for example NGN and USD
  • the central bank being asked

Like any internet request, it also reveals your device’s IP address to that service. It contains nothing you’ve entered in BudT. Rates are saved on your device so the converter works offline.

If you tap a link to a central bank’s website, it opens in your browser, and that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Backups and exports

When you choose Back up data or Export transactions, BudT creates a file and opens your device’s share sheet. You decide where the file goes, for example:

  • your Files app
  • a cloud drive
  • email

Once shared, the file is handled by the service you choose under that service’s terms.

You can protect a backup with a password. The file is then encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-SHA256), so nobody can read it without the password. We can’t recover a forgotten backup password. Backups without a password, and transaction exports (CSV), are not encrypted, so store them somewhere you trust. Restore from backup only reads the file you pick.

Device backups

On Android, BudT opts out of automatic backup to Google Drive, because its encrypted data couldn’t be opened without the key held on the original phone. On iPhone, iCloud or computer backups may include BudT’s encrypted data, depending on your device settings; these backups are managed by Apple, not by us. To move your data to a new phone, use Back up data in BudT.

Deleting your data

To delete your data:

  • Use Settings → Erase all data in the app, or uninstall BudT. Both delete the data from your device.
  • Delete any backup or export files you saved separately.

Children

BudT is not directed at children under 13. It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Financial information disclaimer

Calculator results and exchange rates are estimates for information only, not financial, tax or legal advice.

Changes to this policy

If BudT starts handling data differently, for example by adding optional cloud sync, we’ll update this page and the effective date before the change reaches the app.
